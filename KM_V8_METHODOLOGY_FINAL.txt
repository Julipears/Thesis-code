KM V8 FINAL METHODOLOGY (v8_regular_lm_daily_v4)
=================================================

Intended difference from saved event-store V5
----------------------------------------------
Only shock detection changes: prices are sampled on a complete regular
one-second previous-tick grid and Lee-Mykland is evaluated on one-second log
returns. Resolution, censoring, refractory filtering, and Kaplan-Meier fitting
retain the saved event-store V5 definitions.

Price grid and Lee-Mykland
--------------------------
- Last transaction in each one-second bin, ordered by Binance trade ID.
- Empty seconds use the previous observed transaction price; hence they have a
  zero return. A runtime assertion requires exactly 86,400 ordered timestamps
  in every target UTC day.
- LM is evaluated independently for spot and perpetual returns.
- k = ceil(sqrt(365 * 24 * 60 * 60)) = 5,616 for continuously traded crypto.
- The local BPV estimate contains exactly k-2 = 5,614 preceding BPV products,
  matching movmean(bpv, [k-3 0]) in the supplied MATLAB implementation. These
  products use returns through r(i-1) and reach back 5,615 seconds to r(i-k+1).
- LM log prices are calculated in float64 from the retained last-trade prices.
- Preceding-day data supplies the BPV lookback at the start of each target day.
- n = 86,400 and the extreme-value threshold is recalculated for every UTC day.
- Significance is 0.001, matching V5.
- A detected second is mapped to the final actual origin-market trade in that
  second.

Refractory and resolution (same as saved event-store V5)
--------------------------------------------------------
- Five-second clock-time greedy refractory filter, continuous across midnight.
- Baseline is log(perp)-log(spot) immediately before the mapped shock trade.
- Initial displacement is the absolute basis change at the shock trade.
- Search begins at the next irregular event-time observation.
- Resolution means 90% of the initial displacement has disappeared, subject to
  a 0.5 bp minimum target band and a 0.25 second sustained-entry requirement.
- An origin-market reversal can resolve the basis. This is also how the saved
  event-store V5 module behaves; the responding market is not required to move.
- Maximum follow-up is 120 seconds. Unresolved episodes are right-censored at
  120 seconds, and episodes without a full horizon are excluded.

Kaplan-Meier output
-------------------
- KaplanMeierFitter uses Length as duration and Status as event indicator.
- Monthly comparison graphs pool episode-level observations over the displayed
  date range; they do not average daily KM curves.
- The y-axis is survival probability: probability the basis dislocation remains
  unresolved. Curves include confidence intervals and legend sample sizes.
- V5 comparison rows are restricted to the 90% resolution definition and
  deduplicated by start timestamp.

Audit results
-------------
- Preserved V5 bytecode and the current resolution/refractory implementation
  produced identical outputs in a behavioral equivalence test.
- BTC inverse 2025-07-06 passed the complete-grid check with n=86,400.
- Two independent audit runs produced byte-identical spot and perpetual event
  parquet files after exchange trade-ID ordering was retained.
- Pre-v4 V8 pilot and July 2025 outputs are superseded and must not be used.
