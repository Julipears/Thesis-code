FINAL COVARIATE TIMING CONTRACT
===============================

Existing KM event checkpoints are immutable inputs. No shock/KM rerun.

Daily native-frequency covariates:
- fundingRate_bps: event UTC date - 1 calendar day
- volume: event UTC date - 1 calendar day
- fear_greed: event UTC date - 1 calendar day

Rolling/intraday covariates:
- vol_spot_5min_pct: rolling 5-minute volatility sampled at event_tick - 1
- vol_perp_5min_pct: rolling 5-minute volatility sampled at event_tick - 1
- basis_level_bps: event_tick - 1
- spread_spot_5min: latest completed 1-second-bin rolling observation strictly before start_ts
- spread_perp_5min: latest completed 1-second-bin rolling observation strictly before start_ts
- basis_5min: latest completed 1-second-bin rolling observation strictly before start_ts

The underlying 1-second bid/ask bucket is labeled by its availability time
(bucket start + 1 second), not by its start. Thus an event inside a second can
never receive trades from later in that same second.

Bid/ask trade-side prices are placed on a complete 1-second grid using
previous-tick forward filling before the rolling means are calculated. These
are transaction-side spread proxies, not exchange order-book quoted spreads.

Binance futures metrics (intraday observations stored in daily ZIPs):
- open_interest: latest create_time strictly before start_ts
- trader_long_short: latest create_time strictly before start_ts
- taker_long_short: latest create_time strictly before start_ts

All timestamp as-of joins use direction='backward' and allow_exact_matches=False.
No interpolation is used to construct the event-time price stream.
